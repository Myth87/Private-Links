Private Links Legal Notes

Privacy Policy

This notice describes how Private Links processes information in its Android app and its web app on ChatGPT Sites. Verses publishes this notice at versesbgw.com.

Links you enter or share

Private Links cleans full URLs on your device. On supported URLs it removes known tracking parameters and sharing IDs. When you clean or copy a link, it can write the result to your clipboard.

When you clean a supported Facebook short link, the web app sends the original URL to its resolver on ChatGPT Sites, which asks Facebook for the destination. The original URL can include a share token. The resolver uses the redirect address and does not save a copy in an application database or log. Verses does not receive the URL for this lookup. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

The Android app requests supported short-link redirects directly from the destination platform over HTTPS. The platform receives the original short URL, which can contain a sharing ID, and ordinary connection details such as your IP address. The platform handles that request under its own privacy policy. The Android app does not send these lookups to Verses or ChatGPT Sites.

History and preferences on your device

The app saves cleaned links and timestamps in local storage on your device, along with your chosen language and appearance. The app does not sync them to a Private Links account or cloud database. Use Delete next to an entry or Clear all in History. You can also clear the site’s data in your browser or the app’s storage in Android Settings to remove locally saved preferences and history.

Hosting and sign-in

OpenAI hosts the web app on our behalf through ChatGPT Sites. Access to the hosted app may require ChatGPT sign-in. Our app code does not use your ChatGPT account details. OpenAI may process technical information such as IP addresses, device or browser details, and security or operational logs as part of hosting the app and handling sign-in. The host serving this policy page may also receive standard web request information. See the ChatGPT Sites Data Processing Addendum and OpenAI’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising, analytics and sensitive information

Private Links contains no ads, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics scripts. Do not submit links containing passwords, payment details, medical information, private access tokens, or other confidential information. The service is not directed to children under 13 or children below the applicable minimum age for digital consent.

Your choices and contact

You can delete locally saved data as described above. For privacy questions or to exercise rights that apply to you, use the Verses contact page. If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond to your request. Use OpenAI’s Privacy Portal for requests about an OpenAI account or OpenAI’s own processing.
