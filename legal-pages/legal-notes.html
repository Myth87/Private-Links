Private Links Privacy Policy

Legal Notes

Private Links is an independent tool that removes known tracking parameters and sharing IDs from URLs you supply.

Third-party services

Private Links is not affiliated with or endorsed by the companies whose links it supports. Platform names identify compatible links. Trademarks and linked content belong to their respective owners.

Use links you are entitled to access and share. The destination platform controls who can view content. Its terms and privacy policy apply when you visit it.

Short links and tracking

Private Links automatically looks up supported Facebook short links. Other short links may require you to open them in your browser and paste the destination. The cleaned URL may retain information needed to open the content. Platforms may still identify visits, signed-in users, or shared links through other means.

Availability and your rights
